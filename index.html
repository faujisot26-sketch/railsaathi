Privacy & data status

Know exactly what this prototype can and cannot do.

On-device storage

Journey cards are stored in this browser’s local storage. There is no account or cloud sync.

No PNR collection

Do not enter a real PNR. This prototype does not contact IRCTC or read anyone’s ticket.

No live feed

Train location, delay and platform data are not available here. No simulated live position is shown.

Browser data may be removed when site data is cleared or a different browser/device is used. Do not use this prototype as a substitute for an official ticket or railway announcement.
