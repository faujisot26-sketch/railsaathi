# 3-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

"At the station, the passenger needs a few ticket details quickly. They may have weak internet, but the important information is already on their own ticket. RailSaathi keeps those details readable and available offline."

## 0:25–1:15 — Add a trip

1. Select **Add my journey**.
2. Enter train number and name, boarding station, destination, departure, class, coach, and berth.
3. Save. Show that the trip becomes a readable card immediately.
4. Reload the page and show that the saved trip is still on the device.

Use fictional/sample details during a public demo. Do not use a real passenger name, PNR, or booking reference.

## 1:15–1:50 — Berth guidance

Enter berth **48** for Sleeper and show **Side upper (SU)**. Explain that the guide follows a common layout and the passenger must confirm the official ticket and coach chart.

## 1:50–2:20 — Offline and privacy

Explain that the web app shell can reopen offline after the first online visit and the trip card stays in this browser. Show that export/share is an explicit action. Point to the clear "No live feed / No PNR" status.

## 2:20–3:00 — Honest roadmap

"The next release depends on written railway-data authorization. We will not display fake locations or delays. Once the data agreement is confirmed, we can add a server-side provider integration with freshness indicators, privacy controls, and account deletion."

## Presenter checklist

- Open the hosted HTTPS URL before presenting.
- Use the one-click sample only if the form demonstration needs a faster start; identify it as illustrative.
- Never claim real-time tracking, PNR verification, IRCTC access, or Play Store readiness for this prototype.
- Have a screen recording and screenshots ready in case venue connectivity fails.
